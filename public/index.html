How the call score works

0–100, built to surface established businesses that are easy to reach and missing a real website. Website 30 if none, 24 if only a social or listing page, 4 if it has a site. Reviews up to 30 on a log scale (≈250 reviews maxes it): proof of a busy business. Rating up to 20 (3.0★ = 0, 5.0★ = 20), discounted until ~15 reviews so a lone 5★ can't win. Phone 12, plus 8 if the number looks like a mobile (often the owner directly). No phone caps the score at 40. Hot ≥ 75, Warm ≥ 55.
